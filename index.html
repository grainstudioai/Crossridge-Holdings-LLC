How It Works

A Simpler Way to Sell Your Ohio Property

A traditional listing can make sense for many owners. But it also means preparing the property, scheduling showings, and waiting on buyer financing to fall into place.

We offer a different path. Tell us about the property, and we'll review it against current criteria from investors in our network. If there's a fit, we'll walk you through the transaction — including whether it involves assigning the purchase agreement to an investor — before you decide.

  • Reviewed against real investor criteria
  • Properties considered in any condition
  • Ohio-wide, not just one market
  • Upfront about how the transaction works

Transparency

How We Evaluate Fit For Our Investor Network

We look at recent comparable sales, the property's condition, and current demand from investors in our network. Every property is different, so we don't use a one-size-fits-all formula — and the final terms may come from us or from the investor we assign the agreement to.

We'll explain how we got there, and the transaction structure, before you decide whether to move forward.
